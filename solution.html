Solutions That Work

For every threat, there’s a solution. Here’s how you can help save our oceans.

Actionable Solutions

From personal habits to global policies — every action counts.

Stop Plastic Pollution

Threat: 8–14M tons of plastic enter oceans yearly.
Solution: Use reusable bags, bottles, straws. Support plastic bans.

End Overfishing

Threat: 33% of fish stocks overexploited.
Solution: Buy MSC-certified seafood. Support marine protected areas.

Fight Climate Change

Threat: 90% of coral reefs at risk by 2050.
Solution: Reduce energy use, support renewables, plant mangroves.

Threat: Coastal development destroys ecosystems.
Solution: Support mangrove planting and artificial reefs.

Threat: Fertilizers and toxins create dead zones.
Solution: Support eco-friendly farming and oil spill prevention.

Threat: Non-target species killed in fishing nets.
Solution: Use turtle excluders and circle hooks.
